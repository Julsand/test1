Find your people.
Build something small.

Oslo Hack brings together designers, developers, and curious beginners who want to make things with others. Come alone or bring a friend. Pick an idea, form a small team, and leave with something you built together.

See the session plan
01

Meet

Say hello, share what you are good at, and hear what others want to make. No pitch deck needed.

02

Build

Join a team of two to four people and build something small you can finish in one evening.

03

Share

Show what you made, however rough. Get feedback, swap contacts, and maybe keep going.

How a session works

One evening, four simple parts.

  1. 18:00 Meet Coffee, name tags, quick intros.
  2. 18:30 Pitch ideas One minute each. Teams form around ideas.
  3. 19:00 Build Heads down. Ask for help any time.
  4. 20:30 Share demos Three minutes per team. Applause guaranteed.
